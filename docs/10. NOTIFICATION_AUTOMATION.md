# 10. NOTIFICATION AUTOMATION — DEVI HOSTELS

## 1. Multi-Channel Messaging Infrastructure

DEVI Hostels delivers real-time notifications across three tiers:
1. **WhatsApp Cloud API:** Primary communication channel for Indian college students and airport professionals.
2. **Transactional Email (Resend):** Formal documentation, monthly PDF invoice delivery, and official payment receipts.
3. **SMS Gateway:** Time-critical OTP verification, admission approval alerts, and high-priority escalation notifications.

---

## 2. Automated Rent Reminder Schedule

The billing cron service triggers automated reminders based on the resident's unique joining-date billing anchor:

| Timing | Channel | Template & Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Due Date - 5 Days** | WhatsApp + Email | Advance courtesy reminder: Upcoming rent invoice generated with amount & due date. |
| **Due Date - 2 Days** | WhatsApp | Payment reminder with direct 1-click UPI payment link. |
| **Due Date (0 Days)** | WhatsApp + SMS | Rent due today reminder. |
| **Due Date + 2 Days** | WhatsApp | Gentle overdue notice with late-fee grace policy reminder. |
| **Due Date + 7 Days** | WhatsApp + Admin Alert | 2nd overdue reminder sent to resident; Branch Manager & Accountant notified. |
| **Due Date + 15 Days** | WhatsApp + SMS + Escalation | High-priority escalation; Owner Marpina Pradeep alerted for direct follow-up. |

*Rule:* Reminders stop immediately once `invoice.status = 'paid'`. For partial settlements, notifications reflect only the remaining balance.
